Measurement framework

Measure what we can observe — and label what we infer

1 · VisibilityRecommendation rate, citation rate, prompt-set share of voice, answer sentiment. Stable prompts, repeats, by engine, market and language.
2 · AcquisitionAI referrer sessions, tagged paid clicks, qualified leads and CRM outcomes
3 · Business impactLead quality and incremental margin, assessed against spend and fees
The limit

GA4 referral tracking is incomplete, so we report limitations. Causal lift is claimed only when the experiment design allows it.

So what Agree KPIs and holdouts before the spend, not after.
