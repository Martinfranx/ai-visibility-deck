Where to start

The starting lever depends on three account conditions

GEO

Start here when
  • AI assistants already answer the category
  • The brand is rarely cited, or competitors are
  • There is owned content and data to structure

Ads in AI

Start here when
  • The market and category are open in Ads Manager
  • Landing pages and tracking are ready
  • There is budget for a measured test

Agentic commerce

Start here when
  • The catalogue is structured and complete
  • Checkout is live in the target market
  • A protocol is supported in that region
So what Start with the lever whose conditions are already met. Build the rest in sequence.
